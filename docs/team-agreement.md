# Team agreement

How your team works together. Every member reads it at the start, and you read it again when something goes wrong.

- **How we share the roles:** As the course's default for our team size: six members, each with one role, in three pairs. Task 1 (transactional) is Jean and Martijn, task 2 (informational) is Mauro and Barre, and the content model with releases is Achiel and Lennert.

## How we communicate

- **Where we talk:** One Snapchat group chat for everything about the project.
- **How quickly we answer a teammate:** Within one working day.
- **When and how long we meet:** For 30 minutes once a week.
- **Where we write down what we agreed:** The member with releases and repository (Lennert) writes three lines of notes in the team chat after each meeting.

## How we decide

- **What a role holder decides alone:** Inside a role, the role holder decides and tells the team.
- **What needs the pair:** A change to the content model needs the content-model pair (Achiel and Lennert). Only they create migrations.
- **What needs the whole team:** A change to a user task's steps, or to the brief, needs the whole team.
- **How we settle a disagreement, and when we write a decision record:** If we cannot agree in one meeting, the role holder decides and writes a decision record.

## When a pull request is done

The author of a pull request merges it when all of this is true:

- The author tried the change on its preview, at a narrow width too when something visible changed.
- Its AI note is filled in.
- The starter's checks have finished. The author read what they say, and fixed what a red check found when that was possible.
- A change to the content model has its migration. Only the content-model pair creates migrations.

We ask a teammate for a review when the author is not sure of a change.

## The AI note

Every pull request has the section *AI note: what I asked AI, and what I checked*, under that exact heading. The note is one or two lines and has two parts: *What I asked AI* and *What I checked*. When no AI tool was used, the note says "No AI used".

- **How we check what an AI tool wrote before we use it:** We try the change ourselves on the preview with made-up details, and we read the code the AI wrote before we commit it.

## When someone stops contributing

- **What counts as stopping for us:** No answer for three working days, or a task still open a week after its date.
- **What the pair does first:** The pair partner contacts the member directly.
- **What the whole team does next:** We discuss it at the weekly meeting and agree a new date and, if needed, a smaller task.
- **How we record what happened:** We change this agreement when we share the roles or the work again.
